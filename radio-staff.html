    Staff tools

    Listen live

    Opens the radio in monitor mode: you hear every channel and can't talk.

    Listen live

    The event channel shows on every radio as CH 4 while it's open. Closing it moves everyone on it back to CH 1.

    Channel access by rank

    Pick which Melonly department ranks can use each channel. A channel with nothing ticked is open to everyone. CH 1 is always open, and admins can use every channel. Department and rank also show next to callsigns on the radios.

    Scene timers 0

    How long each unit has been in a traffic stop or scene voice channel. Dispatch (!C) sees the same on their radio under SCENES.

      Flags 0

      Things the radio noticed for staff to check, like a Civilian-team player on the police radio. Kept for 24 hours.

        Recordings

        Every radio transmission and everything said in the Discord radio channel from the last 24 hours, for reviewing reports. Older ones are deleted.

          Crash reports 0

          Errors sent in by radios (desktop and web) in the last 7 days.

            Muted, not on a radio 0

              Mute someone else

              Find a member by name or Discord ID to mute them before they sign in to the radio.

                Signal coverage

                Place cell towers and draw weak-signal areas, like deep in the park or High Rock. A unit's signal comes from where they are in game: near a tower is clear, far from every tower or inside a weak area sounds static-y and muffled on the radio, but still comes through. With no towers, only the weak areas change anything. Discord voice is not affected.
