FAQ

Do I apply here or in Discord? Either works. This page submits the same application for command review.

Can I have two pending applications? No. Finish or wait for review before applying again.

What if I am denied? You can re-apply after three days. The status card on this page will show denied until then.

What happens after approval? Complete training and an R/A with Field Training Operations.
