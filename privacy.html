Privacy Policy

Last updated August 11, 2026

This policy explains what Clearwater Roleplay collects when you use the website and Clearwater Internet.

Information we use

  • Discord profile data needed to sign you in, such as your username, display name, avatar, and server membership.
  • Posts, messages, likes, follows, and other Internet activity you choose to create.
  • Hashed network identifiers used to enforce bans and reduce abuse. These are not stored as raw IP addresses in public pages.
  • Staff notes, reports, warnings, and automod holds related to moderation.

How it is used

We use this information to run the community feed, keep Discord membership tied to Internet access, enforce rules, and operate the staff panel. Public pages show hashed user identifiers rather than raw Discord snowflakes wherever possible.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Content you post is visible to other Clearwater Internet users according to your privacy settings. Staff can review reported or automod-held content. Discord receives data only as part of the official sign-in process.

Retention

Account data stays while your account is active. Deleted accounts remove associated posts and messages from the Internet store. Moderation logs may be kept as needed to enforce bans and prevent evasion.

Your choices

You can hide profile media, restrict messages, deactivate, or delete your Clearwater Internet account from Settings. Signing out of Discord on this site ends the website session.

Contact

Privacy questions can be raised with Clearwater staff in Discord.
