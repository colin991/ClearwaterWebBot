About sponsored placements

Clearwater departments and businesses can buy 48-hour sponsored slots in the sidebar, feed, or Reels with Clearwater Credits. Staff review every ad before it goes live.

  • Sidebar and feed start at C$1200 for 48 hours. Reel pricing scales with video length. Optional boosts raise how often the ad is shown.
  • Ads must promote an in-game department or business — not Discord invites, Roblox groups, or off-platform spam.
  • Use Account on a sponsored card to open the advertiser’s Clearwater profile.
  • If an ad is misleading, harassing, or breaks the rules, report it below so staff can review it.
