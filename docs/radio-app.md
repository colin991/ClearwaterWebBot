# Clearwater Radio (Windows app + radio bot)

A push-to-talk radio overlay for Windows that talks through a second Discord bot.

## Pieces

- `radio-app/`: Electron app. Frameless, always on top, styled like a handheld radio.
- `utils/radioBot.js`: the second bot. No commands. Logs in with `RADIO_BOT_TOKEN`, joins voice channel `1514128904783139018`, and rejoins if it is dropped or moved.
- `utils/radioRelay.js`: WebSocket relay at `/radio`. Apps send Opus audio while PTT is held (faded in and out, ending on a silent frame, so a quick tap does not pop); the bot plays it in the channel, and drops transmissions under 80 ms (`createTransmitSink` in `utils/radioBot.js`). Everyone talking in the channel (Discord users and other apps) is sent back to every app.
- `utils/radioDownloadPanel.js`: the main bot keeps a download embed in channel `1514190592005902336` with a **Download for Windows** link.
- `.github/workflows/radio-app.yml`: builds the installer on Windows. Every push to `main` that changes `radio-app/` publishes release `radio-v1.0.<run>`; installed apps download it and restart on the new version by themselves.

## Access

When the radio opens without a sign-in (first launch, or an expired or revoked one) it opens Discord sign-in in the browser by itself; if that window is closed, OK tries again (or MENU → SETUP → ACCOUNT). The app opens Discord in the browser and catches the redirect on `http://127.0.0.1:53682/callback`; the relay exchanges the code with the client secret, checks the member, and hands back a signed token the app keeps. Only members of the Clearwater server who can view and connect to the radio channel get in. Every 5 minutes the relay re-checks everyone on the radio; anyone who left the server or lost access is disconnected, signed out, and their app closes. Only one radio can transmit at a time on each channel; others get a busy tone.

## Radio controls

The radio looks like a handheld: volume and channel knobs on top, a color screen (zone, channel, date and time, and the units on your channel), a settings (gear) key and a home key, a navigation ring with OK and back, and a lettered keypad.

- **PTT**: hold the bound key or mouse button (works while a game is focused), or hold the long PTT bar on the left edge. On first launch (or with no talk key saved) the desktop radio shows `PICK A TALK KEY` and stays there until a key or mouse 4/5 is pressed; then shows the SIZE list (up/down previews it, OK or a click keeps it), and signs in after that. The web radio skips this and uses its HOLD TO TALK bar.
- **VOL** knob: drag up/down or scroll. On the home screen the navigation ring also changes volume.
- **CH** knob: a dial like VOL; drag it up/down or scroll to turn it, or click to step to the next channel. It points at the current channel. CH 1 is the Discord radio channel; CH 2 is **Special Response Team** and CH 3 is **Frequency Change**; both are radio-to-radio only and never reach Discord (they work even when the bot is out of voice). The radio remembers your last channel.
- **PTT spam**: pressing PTT 6 times within 10 seconds times that member out of talking on the radio for 1 minute (all channels; Discord itself is untouched). The relay enforces it, even across reconnects, and the radio screen counts the minute down.
- **Red side button** (under PTT): deafen yourself (stops incoming audio). While deafened you can't talk either: PTT gives the busy tone and the screen shows `DEAFENED · NO TALK` (the relay refuses it too, and deafening mid-transmission ends it).
- **Gear key** (top left of the keys, was P1): SETTINGS with `VOL` (listening volume), `TX VOL` (how loud you are on the radio), `MIC`, `LISTEN` (speakers or headset you hear the radio on), `PTT`, `CHANNEL`, `WHO` (everyone on each channel, app radios and the Discord radio voice channel together, marked APP, DISCORD VC or APP + VC; click a channel's header twice, or pick it and press OK, to switch to that channel), `FX` (listening effect RADIO/POLICE/OFF; RADIO by default and never applied to what you send) and `SETUP` (`TEST` to hear your own radio voice, `ACCOUNT` to sign in or out with Discord, `UPDATE`). Up/down picks a row, left/right changes VOL, CHANNEL and FX, and OK selects (or opens the menu from the home screen). From any settings page the gear key goes straight back to the radio screen. Device names show in full.
- **Home key** (was P2): back to the radio screen from anywhere. On the radio screen it opens the full list of units on every channel (the same list as SETTINGS → WHO). The radio screen itself lists the units on your channel, marked APP, VC or APP+VC.
- **One voice at a time**: only one radio can transmit per channel (the radio shows `BUSY` and their name). Talking in the Discord radio channel does not block the radios.
- **In the Discord channel**: if you are already sitting in the Discord radio voice channel, your app mutes itself (`IN VC` on the screen) so you don't hear everything twice, and push-to-talk does nothing (it shows `IN DISCORD VC`), so you talk in Discord instead. If you join mid-transmission, it stops. It all turns back on when you leave or switch channels. Nothing changes in Discord.
- **Panic**: press the blue **PANIC** button on the radio (it asks `ARE YOU SURE?`; press PANIC again or OK within 5 seconds to send, anything else cancels), or type `;panic [location]` in game chat. Every signed-in radio flashes red for 10 seconds with who it is and where, plays the panic sound twice (`radio-app/renderer/panic-sound.js`), and a voice says "<unit> panic at postal <postal>" (Windows' built-in text to speech, on the radios only, never in Discord). The unit number is the player's ER:LC callsign, or for the PANIC button a number at the start of their Discord nickname ("1A-12 | Name"); without one the voice says their name. Without a typed location the bot looks the player up in the live ER:LC player list (the PANIC button uses the member's linked Roblox account or their Discord name) and shows their street or postal. One PANIC press per member every 2 minutes (the relay enforces it, even across reconnects); pressing it sooner shows `PANIC WAIT m:ss`. ER:LC events or emergency calls marked as a panic also trigger it; ER:LC's public API has no panic-button event. The bot logs `Radio: panic from …` or `Radio: EMER from …` (the PANIC button) each time.
- **FX LVL** (SETTINGS row after FX): how strong the listening effect is, 25 to 150% (100% is the normal RADIO/POLICE sound). Lower opens the sound toward clean audio; higher is narrower, grittier and has more static.
- **SIZE** (SETTINGS row after POWER): a dial from 60% to 130% of full size in 5% steps (default 100%). The arrows on the highlighted row step it; OK opens the dial, where up/down, left/right, the on-screen arrows, scrolling, or dragging or clicking the dial set it, and OK saves. First launch uses the same dial. The desktop radio keeps its bottom-right corner in place (a bigger radio never goes off the top or left of the screen) and remembers the size. On the web radio, sizes above 100% let the page scroll.
- **Mouse**: every button on the radio clicks, and so does the screen: click a settings row to open or change it, the ◀ ▶ arrows on the highlighted row to turn VOL, TX VOL, CHANNEL, FX, POWER or SIZE, a device to pick it, and the SETTINGS / UNITS / HOME labels along the bottom of the screen.
- **POWER** (SETTINGS row after FX): `AUTO` (default) runs in low power on PCs with 4 or fewer CPU cores or 4 GB or less memory, `LOW` always, `FULL` never. Low power uses a lighter Opus encoder and no listening-effect oversampling, and updates the mic lamp less often. In every mode the listening effect and its static only run while they can be heard.
- **Dispatch status**: the channel card on the radio screen shows `DISPATCH ACTIVE` (green) or `DISPATCH OFFLINE` (red). Active means someone with `!C` in their name is in the Discord radio voice channel or signed in on a radio. That's the same rule as the voice channel's "Dispatch Online" status. It updates within 15 seconds.
- **Dispatch priority**: someone with `!C` in their name can talk over anyone who isn't dispatch. Their PTT cuts the current talker off (that radio shows `DISPATCH PRIORITY`); nobody can cut in over dispatch.
- **Last heard**: the channel card shows `LAST HEARD <name> · <time>` for the last transmission on your channel (radio or Discord).
- **Who is talking**: in the units lists (home screen and WHO'S ON), whoever is talking moves to the top of their channel in green, on a radio or in the Discord channel.
- **SCAN** (SETTINGS row after CHANNEL): pick up to two other channels to hear as well as your own. You always talk on your own channel; voices from a scanned channel show as `RX NAME (CH 2)`.
- **Mic check and tour**: first launch goes talk key, then a REPLAY KEY and a CH KEY (OK, BACK or Esc skips each), then size, a mic check with the level meter (`SETUP → TEST` shows the same meter any time), then a 7-page tour (`SETUP → TUTORIAL` shows it again). Radios set up before the tour, and the web radio on its first visit, show the tour once.
- **Callsign**: the title bar shows the unit number at the start of your Discord nickname (`1A-12 | Name` shows `Z1 · 1A-12`), or your name without one.
- **Hotkeys** (SETTINGS): `SCAN PTT` is a second talk key that talks on your first SCAN channel, `CH KEY` switches to the next channel, `REPLAY KEY` plays the last 30 seconds of radio again (press again to stop; `SETTINGS → REPLAY` does the same). Backspace while setting one clears it. The desktop keys work while a game has focus; on the web radio they work while the page is focused.
- **Stuck mic**: a transmission (or whisper) held for 60 seconds is cut, and the radio shows `STUCK MIC · CUT`. The relay enforces it too.
- **Whisper**: on WHO'S ON, click a unit twice (or pick it and press OK), then `WHISPER`. Your talk key now talks only to that unit's radio (`WHISPER NAME · PTT IS PRIVATE · BACK ENDS`); it never reaches Discord or the channel. BACK on the radio screen ends it, and it ends on its own after a minute unused. Only units on a radio app can be whispered to.
- **Dispatch move**: a `!C` dispatcher picks a unit on WHO'S ON and chooses `MOVE TO CH n`. That radio switches channel and shows `DISPATCH MOVED YOU`. Only `!C` names can do it (the relay checks).
- **911 pending**: the radio screen lists everyone waiting in the Discord 911 voice channel (`RADIO_911_CHANNEL_ID`, default 1532197874690035843) with how long they've waited. In-game 911 calls are not listed. A new one plays a short tone.
- **Panic map**: a panic with a postal shows a mini Liberty County map (`radio-app/renderer/map-image.js`) with a pulsing marker on the postal and `POSTAL nnn` big underneath.
- **NIGHT** (SETTINGS): dims the screen and keys for dark games. **AUTO GAIN** (SETTINGS, on by default): lifts quiet mics toward a normal level (up to 6x, never louder than the limiter allows).
- **Talk pop-up**: while the desktop radio is minimized or hidden, a small pop-up in the bottom-right corner shows who is talking. On the web radio the tab title shows it, plus a notification if the browser allows them.
- **Settings sync**: VOL, TX VOL, FX, FX LVL, SCAN, NIGHT and AUTO GAIN are saved on the bot per member (`data/radio-settings.json`), so the desktop and phone radios share them; the newest change wins. Keys, mic, speakers and size stay per device.
- **Looks**: the signal bars sweep while connecting and bounce while someone talks; the battery has a slow shine.
- **Talk log**: every radio transmission (desktop or web, any channel) posts a line in the radio voice channel's chat: `@member talked from 14:35:34-14:35:57`, with ` on Special Response Team` or ` on Frequency Change` for the radio-only channels. Times are Discord timestamps, so each reader sees their own time zone. The mention never pings. Lines are batched for a couple of seconds. The main bot posts them, with the radio bot as fallback; `RADIO_LOG_CHANNEL_ID` moves them to another channel. Talking in Discord itself is not logged.
- **Auto-close**: if nobody presses PTT or touches the radio for 20 minutes, it shows `CLOSING IN 60s` and then shuts off; any key keeps it on.
- **Startup**: turning the radio on shows the Clearwater logo, a loading bar and the version for about 2 seconds.
- **Reconnecting and updating**: if the link drops, the screen shows `RECONNECTING` and retries 3 times (1, 2 and 4 seconds). If the bot is still unreachable it shows `RADIO UPDATING · PLEASE WAIT` with a countdown and tries again every 10 seconds until it's back. Settings are kept the whole time.
- **Talk timer**: while transmitting, the screen counts how long you've been talking (`TRANSMITTING · 0:12`).
- **Game line**: the channel card shows your nearest postal, street, speed and heading from the ER:LC player list (`PST 204 · MAIN ST · 35 MPH NE`), updated every 5 seconds. ER:LC's API has no speed or heading, so both are estimated from how far you moved between updates. It's hidden while you're not in game.
- **Weather alert**: when the server weather (the bot's weather roller) turns to rain or a thunderstorm, radios play a tone, flash `WEATHER ALERT` and show `⚠ RAIN · ROADS ARE WET` on the channel card until it clears.
- **WHERE AM I** (SETTINGS): reads your nearest postal and street aloud (local text to speech, only on your radio).
- **NEAREST** (SETTINGS): the 3 closest other units on a radio who are in game, with their postal and distance in miles. Display only; nobody is pinged.
- **Radio damage**: when the ER:LC kill log shows you were downed, your radio cracks (a cracked, flickering screen with `RADIO DAMAGED` and a countdown, and one signal bar) for 20 seconds. Everyone hears you very weak, and you hear everyone weak, until it recovers.
- **Off team**: if you're on the Civilian team in game while on the radio (two updates in a row), your radio says `OFF TEAM · STAFF HAVE BEEN TOLD` out loud and on screen, the radio staff page lists a flag, and a note is posted in the radio channel chat.
- **Recorded transmissions**: every radio transmission (and whisper) and everything said in the Discord radio channel is recorded for staff to review reports, and deleted after 24 hours. Each radio shows a one-time `RECORDED` notice the first time it signs in.
- **Staff warning**: a pop-up from staff (`STAFF WARNING` and their message) stays on the radio screen until you press OK.
- **Staff announcement**: staff can play a short text announcement on every radio. It shows on screen and is read aloud by the computer's own voice (free text to speech, never in Discord).
- **Event channel**: while staff have it open, a temporary channel shows as CH 4 on every radio. When they close it, everyone on it moves back to CH 1 (`EVENT CLOSED · BACK ON CH 1`).
- **SCENES** (SETTINGS, dispatch `!C` names only): how long each unit has been in a traffic stop or scene voice channel (`RADIO_SCENE_CHANNEL_IDS`).
- **MONITOR** (SETTINGS, admins only): hear every channel at once, listen-only. The bot checks Administrator live.
- **Crash reports**: if the app or web radio hits an error, it is sent to the radio staff page (right away, or on the next sign-in if the radio was offline). The desktop radio reloads its screen by itself if it crashes.
- **PRACTICE** (SETTINGS): a test mode for new users. PTT records you (up to 30 seconds) without going live, and releasing plays it back through the radio sound. The screen shows `PRACTICE MODE · NOT LIVE`; pick PRACTICE again to turn it off.
- `#` and OK select, `*` and the back key go back. **QUIT** shuts the app off (installing a downloaded update first). The app checks for updates on launch, when reopened, and every 10 minutes; `%APPDATA%\Clearwater Radio\update.log` records any update error.

## Staff tools

These post in the radio voice channel's chat with the talk log (or `RADIO_LOG_CHANNEL_ID` if set):

- **Radio staff panel**: [cwpcso.com/radio-staff](https://www.cwpcso.com/radio-staff) (sign in with Discord), or `/radio-staff` in Discord, which also links to the page. Only members with **Administrator** can open it. It lists who is on the radio and who is force-muted; pick a member to **Force mute** (they can listen but not talk, and it survives restarts, saved in `data/radio-admin.json`), **Unmute**, or **Kick off radio** (closes their radio; they can't sign back in for 5 minutes). Each action is noted in the channel. On the website, admins can also find a member by name or Discord ID to mute them before they sign in. The bot checks Administrator live on every request (`/api/radio/staff` on the bot API, reached through `/api/bot/radio-staff` on the site).
- **Signal coverage** (cell towers and weak areas): on the website staff panel, admins place cell towers (drag to move, a range slider) and draw weak-signal areas (corners on the map, a "signal inside" slider), then **Save coverage**. Saved in `data/radio-coverage.json`. Every 5 seconds the bot finds each radio user in the ER:LC player list (linked Roblox account, else their name) and works out their signal: clear within 60% of a tower's range, fading past it, and multiplied down inside a weak area, never below 10% so transmissions always come through. Not in game means clear; with no towers only the weak areas matter. App and web radios hear a weak talker muffled with static and short dropouts (FX on or off), and each radio's bars show its own signal. Discord voice stays clean. The staff map also shows where each radio user is, coloured by signal.
- **Building interference** (tunnels and parking garages): on the coverage map, **+ Tunnel / garage** draws a purple area (25% signal inside by default). They only count while the **Building interference** box is ticked (off by default); then Save coverage.
- **Flags**: the staff page lists things the radio noticed in the last 24 hours: a Civilian-team player on the police radio, or someone who talked over dispatch 3 times in 10 minutes (pressing PTT while a `!C` dispatcher has the channel, talking on CH 1 while a dispatcher speaks in the Discord channel, or speaking in the Discord channel over a dispatcher). Each flag is also noted in the radio channel chat.
- **Staff tools** on the website: **Warn** next to a unit (pop-up on their radio), **Listen live** (opens the web radio in monitor mode: every channel, listen-only), **Play on all radios** (a text announcement read aloud), **Open / Close event channel** (CH 4), **Scene timers**, **Recordings** (the last 24 hours, play in the browser, filter by name; saved in `data/radio-recordings/`), and **Crash reports** from radios (last 7 days, `data/radio-crashes.json`). Warnings, announcements and the event channel are noted in the radio channel chat; playing a recording is logged on the bot.
- **Panic history**: every panic (radio PANIC button or in game) with who, unit, where, postal and when.
- **Weekly radio stats**: every Monday 00:00 UTC, time on the radio, transmissions and talk time per member for the past week (`data/radio-stats.json`).

## Web radio (phones)

`cwpcso.com/radio` is the desktop radio itself in a browser: the site build (`scripts/build-site.js`) turns `radio-app/renderer/index.html`, `radio.js`, `radio.css`, the worklet and the panic sound into `public/radio.html`, and `radio-web-shim.js` stands in for the Electron bridge (settings in the browser, the website's sign-in, the relay address). So it shows exactly what the app shows: zone, channel, date and time, units, settings, FX, TX VOL, POWER, WHO and panic. `radio-web.css` adds Clearwater branding around it, scales the handheld to the screen, and puts a big HOLD TO TALK bar under it for phones. QUIT or 20 minutes idle turns it off until you tap Turn on radio. Sign-in is the website's Discord sign-in. Clearwater members who don't have website access get a radio-only cookie that opens nothing else on the site. The page asks `/api/bot/radio` for a radio key; that asks the bot (`/api/radio/web-session`), which returns a key and the relay's secure address. The relay checks Clearwater membership as usual, and one radio per member still applies, so signing in on a phone signs the desktop app out.

HTTPS pages can't use the relay's plain `ws://` address, so the bot starts a free Cloudflare quick tunnel to its own port on boot (`utils/radioTunnel.js` downloads `cloudflared` into `data/bin`) and hands out its `wss://` address. The address changes when the bot restarts; the website always asks for the current one. Set `RADIO_WEB_URL=wss://…/radio` to use your own HTTPS domain instead (also add it to `connect-src` in the site's Content-Security-Policy in `vercel.json`, which allows `wss://*.trycloudflare.com`), or `RADIO_WEB_TUNNEL=off` to turn it off. The page needs WebCodecs audio: current Chrome, Edge, Android browsers and Safari on iOS 26 or newer. Keep the page open on a phone; it reconnects when you come back to it.

## Host setup

See `.env.example` (`RADIO_BOT_TOKEN`, `RADIO_PUBLIC_URL`, optional `RADIO_PORT`, and `DISCORD_CLIENT_ID` + `DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET` or `RADIO_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID` + `RADIO_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET` for sign-in). Add `http://127.0.0.1:53682/callback` as an OAuth2 redirect on that Discord application. The radio bot needs View Channel, Connect, and Speak in the radio channel. The app connects to `ws://216.173.77.2:8102/radio` (`DEFAULT_RELAY_URL` in `radio-app/main.js`).
