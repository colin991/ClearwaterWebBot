# Police car whitelist

Enabled on bot startup. Players on the **Police** team who spawn a car that is not one of these models are PMed in-game and DMed on Discord:

- Falcon Advance XET
- 2015 Bullhorn Prancer
- 2021 Chevlon Camion
- 2024 FPIU (also matches `2024 Falcon Interceptor Utility`)

The in-game PM says to switch cars and check Discord DMs. The Discord DM lists the allowed cars. If they still have a blocked car, the in-game PM repeats every **3 minutes**. Exempt staff are skipped. Sheriff, Fire, DOT, and civilians are not checked.
