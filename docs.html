Clearwater Internet V.2

Clearwater Internet is a community platform similar to X or Instagram where members can communicate with posts, messages, Reels, replies, and more. After two months of work, it is ready for public release inside Clearwater Roleplay.

Discord members only

This website is strictly for members of the Clearwater Roleplay Discord server. Do not advertise other servers here.

Getting started

When you enter the website, you land on the home page with the active player count. From there you can open Departments or Internet. On Internet, set up your account in Settings, then start posting from Home.

Home

On Home you can use:

  • For You — recommended and trending content
  • Recent — the newest posts
  • Following — posts from people you follow
  • Official — posts from the official Clearwater account

Creating a post

Start typing in the composer. You can:

  • Add a GIF with the GIF button, then remove it with Remove
  • Add an image with the image icon, then remove it with Remove
  • Add an emoji with the emoji button
  • Mention someone by typing @ or using the @ button
  • Create a poll with the three-line icon, add a question, 1–4 options, and a duration in days

When you are ready, click Post.

Post actions

Use the three dots to report a post. Like with the heart, bookmark with the bookmark icon, and share with the share button.

Right sidebar

  • Search people and posts
  • What’s happening — trending hashtags
  • Sponsored ads with Learn, Account, and report options
  • Who to follow — suggested accounts

Reels

Open Reels from the left rail, then click Post a Reel.

  • Upload photos for a slideshow (you can add music), or upload one video up to 2 GB
  • Write a caption, then upload
  • On slideshow Reels, click to move between images
  • On Reels with audio, use the slider to mute, unmute, or change volume

You can follow the creator, report from the three dots, like, comment, or share. On the far right you may see Learn more, sponsored ads, and Who to watch.

Wallet

Wallet has five tabs: Overview, Advertise, Market, Levels, and Send.

Overview

Clearwater Credits (C$) are the roleplay currency. Check your balance and daily credits here. Most members receive C$75 every 24 hours; donors, contributors, and staff earn more. Transactions lists credits you gained or spent.

Advertise

Buy sidebar, feed, or Reel ads. You need an approved business account. Fill out the form and submit for staff review for C$1,200. Boost chances can increase visibility. Reel ad pricing depends on video length.

Market

Buy Clearwater Credits with Robux in the credit store. Link your Roblox account with Verify first, then use Buy on Roblox.

Levels

Chat and level up for an easier way to earn credits. Track your level and progress here.

Send

Send or request credits. Choose Send or Request, enter the member, amount, and optional note. Bank updates arrive in Messages.

Notifications & Messages

Notifications cover likes, replies, reposts, quotes, follows, and more.

In Messages, click New message to start a conversation. Add text, a GIF, or an emoji. Report abusive messages by reporting the account so staff can review.

Bookmarks

Create collection names, then click Create. Bookmark posts from Home. Collections help you organize saved posts.

Profile & Settings

Your public profile shows posts, replies, mentions, media, and likes. Open Following or Followers for connection lists. Use Copy link for your share URL (/profiles/username).

Settings

  • Profile — banner (gallery or upload), accent color, bio, and pinned post
  • Privacy — seven privacy controls, including private likes
  • Appearance — display preferences
  • Account — verification application, business account application, deactivate, or delete

Deactivating hides your profile while keeping data. Deleting removes your account data except records we must keep for legal and safety reasons.
