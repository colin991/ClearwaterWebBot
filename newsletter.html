Aug 14 Version V.2

Version V.2

Public launch of Clearwater Internet

After two months of work, Clearwater Internet is live for Clearwater Roleplay Discord members — a community feed for posts, messages, and more.

Features

  • Home feed — For You, Recent, Following, and Official tabs with GIF, image, emoji, mention, poll, and location tools.
  • Wallet — Clearwater Credits, daily drops, ads, Roblox credit store, levels, and send/request.
  • Social inbox — Notifications for likes, replies, and follows, plus direct messages with GIF and emoji support.
  • Profiles — Public profiles, privacy controls, accent colors, banners, and shareable /profiles/username links.

Improvements

  • Member-only access — Sign-in requires the Clearwater Roleplay Discord server. Do not advertise other servers here.
  • Bookmarks & settings — Collections, verification and business applications, and account deactivate/delete controls.
  • Docs & newsletter — Full guide at /docs; this page stays a light update log.
